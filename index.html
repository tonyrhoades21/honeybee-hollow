Welcome to Honeybee Hollow

Providing safe, nurturing, and high-quality childcare in Locust Fork, Alabama.

About Us

Honeybee Hollow is a nature-themed daycare serving children from 6 weeks to 12 years old. Our mission is to create a warm, safe, and engaging environment where children can learn, grow, and thrive.

We focus on development, creativity, and building strong foundations for lifelong learning.

Our Programs

Infant Care (6 weeks - 18 months)

Individualized care including feeding, naps, and sensory development.

Toddler Care (18 months - 3 years)

Language development, play-based learning, and toilet training support.

Preschool (3 - 5 years)

Early learning including reading, math, science, and kindergarten readiness.

Before & After School (K–6)

  • Transportation included
  • Homework help
  • Snacks and activities
